# 关于本站 / About this blog

这里是 AI Native Notes，记录 AI Native 时代的技术笔记与项目实践。

我希望把学习和实践中的问题、尝试、验证过程与经验整理成可回顾的文章。内容以中文和英文写作，共用文章列表，通过标签组织。

This is AI Native Notes, a place for technical notes and project practice in the AI-native era.

The focus is on questions, experiments, verification, and lessons learned. Posts are written in Chinese or English and organized with tags in one shared feed.

## 内容方向 / Topics

- **AI Native**：AI 时代的工具、工作方式与思考 / Tools, workflows, and reflections.
- **工程实践**：实现过程、问题排查与验证 / Implementation, debugging, and validation.
- **项目记录**：项目进展、设计取舍与复盘 / Progress, design decisions, and retrospectives.

语言标签使用 `中文` 或 `English`。站点提供 RSS 订阅。

Language tags are `中文` and `English`. RSS is available for following new posts.
